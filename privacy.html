Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026.

What this site is

Granny Recipes lets people read recipes and, with an account, post recipes, comment, save favorites, and rate. The shop describes products that are not on sale yet.

Demo mode

If Supabase is not configured, data stays in this browser's localStorage. That includes your email, a hash of your password, recipes, comments, hearts, ratings, and reports. It is not sent to a server by this site. Anyone who can use your browser profile can read that storage. Clearing site data removes it.

Supabase mode

If the public config file contains a Supabase project URL and anon key, accounts, recipes, comments, hearts, ratings, reports, and photos are stored in that Supabase project. Supabase processes authentication. Passwords are handled by Supabase, not by this page. The anon key is public; access rules in the database are what limit reading and writing. The owner can read reports and hidden items.

What other people see

Your display name is shown with recipes and comments. Your email is not shown on the public page. Hearts and ratings are counted. Other visitors are not shown a list of who clicked them.

Shop

"Coming soon / Notify me" opens your email app with a draft message. Nothing is sent until you send it. If a Stripe Payment Link is added later, card payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's site. This static site does not receive card numbers. A PDF download linked from the thank-you page is a file on the public site unless you host it somewhere else.

Cookies

Demo mode uses localStorage, not a tracking cookie. Supabase Auth stores a session in browser storage. Advertising is off while the ad provider is set to none, so the advertising-cookie banner stays hidden and no ad script is loaded.

Advertising cookies

If the site owner later sets the ad provider to AdSense in assets/js/ads-config.js, a consent banner appears before any advertising cookie is set. Accepting allows the ad script to load. Rejecting keeps the ad slots empty. The choice is stored in this browser under a local key and is not an account setting. Ad slots are kept out of print views, the recipe form, the login page, and shop checkout. See the Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure.

Affiliates

A recipe can list affiliate links. Those links use rel="sponsored nofollow", and the page shows a short disclosure when any are present. The other site may learn that you arrived from Granny Recipes. Affiliate links are optional, off until configured, and are not a sale of your account information.

Children

The site is not directed at children, and photos of children are not allowed.
