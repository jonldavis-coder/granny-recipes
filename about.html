About Granny Recipes

Granny Recipes is a small website for recipes people want to keep: the Sunday roast, the biscuits, the pie that always came out of the same oven. It is a community cookbook, not a biography of any real person.

The recipes already on the shelf are labeled Sample recipe. They are generic home-cooking notes written for this site. They are not quotations, and they are not attributed to a named person. The pictures are simple illustrations made for the site, not photographs of real people or children.

How saving works

Until a Supabase project is connected, the site runs in demo mode. The banner says so: your posts are saved only in this browser. Create an account with email and password, or use the one-time sign-in link shown on the login page. Hearts, stars, and comments stay on this device too.

When config.js has a Supabase URL and anon key, the same screens use that shared kitchen instead. Setup steps are in the README.

The shop

Granny's Kitchen Shop is marked Opening soon. Recipe cards, a card box, a gift set, and The Granny Recipes Book are described there. Any prices you see are placeholders, not final prices, and an empty checkout slot does not take payment.

Moderation

Every recipe and comment has a report button. The owner can hide or delete posts and comments. In demo mode, set OWNER_EMAIL in config.js. With Supabase, add your user to public.owners.
