Community guidelines

This kitchen works when people are decent to each other. These are the house rules.

  • Be kind. Disagree about salt if you must, but do not insult, harass, or target anyone.
  • Post recipes you have a right to share. Do not paste a book, a paid class, or someone else's story as if it were yours.
  • Do not publish another person's private details, full name, address, or contact information.
  • Do not upload photos of real people or children. Food and handwritten cards are enough.
  • No spam, no pile of links, and no comments that are only an advertisement.
  • Do not post dangerous instructions. Recipes are home cooking, not medical advice.
  • Use the report button when something breaks these rules. The owner may hide or delete it.
  • The shop is not open. Do not send card numbers or payment details by email. "Notify me" only asks to be told when something is available.

Sample recipes are starting points. If you cook one, you are welcome to say how it went — in your own words, without inventing a quote from a real person.
