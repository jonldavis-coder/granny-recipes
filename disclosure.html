Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

Granny Recipes is a community cookbook. The shelves and the shop can be read without an account. Advertising space is built in, and it is empty.

Advertising

The ad provider in assets/js/ads-config.js is none. Empty slots are not drawn and do not take space. If that provider is later set to AdSense, and a publisher ID and slot IDs are filled in, ads can appear in these places:

  • Below the recipe introduction
  • Between the steps of a long recipe
  • A sidebar on a wide screen
  • A dismissible bar at the bottom of a phone
  • In the recipe browse grid, after every sixth card

Ads are not placed on the print view, the ingredients shopping list, the post-a-recipe form, the login page, or shop checkout. A consent banner asks before any advertising cookie is set. Until you accept, the ad script is not loaded. You can read the privacy draft for how that choice is stored.

The root ads.txt file is a commented placeholder. It does not name a seller until the owner publishes a real publisher ID.

Affiliates

Affiliate links are off until they are listed in the same config file. When a recipe uses one, the link is marked rel="sponsored nofollow" and the page says that a purchase through it may earn this site a commission, at no extra cost to you. Sample recipes do not include affiliate links.

The shop

Granny's Kitchen Shop is opening soon. Its prices are placeholders. Checkout, when it exists, is handled by Stripe and is not an ad slot.
