Sunday Pot Roast

A covered-pot supper that fills the kitchen while it cooks. The vegetables go in later so they stay in distinct pieces.

25 min prep · 3 hr cook · Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds beef chuck roast
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 large onion, cut in wedges
  • 4 garlic cloves, smashed
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 sprigs thyme, or 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 1/2 pounds potatoes, cut in chunks
  • 4 carrots, cut in chunks

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Pat the roast dry and season it with salt and pepper.
  2. Warm the oil in a heavy Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown the roast on all sides, then set it on a plate.
  3. Lower the heat. Cook the onion until the edges brown. Stir in the garlic and tomato paste for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the broth and Worcestershire sauce and scrape up the browned bits. Return the roast to the pot, add the thyme and bay leaf, and cover.
  5. Roast 2 hours. Add the potatoes and carrots, cover again, and roast 45 to 60 minutes more, until a fork slides into the meat easily.
  6. Rest 15 minutes. Skim the fat from the juices, taste for salt, and serve the meat with the vegetables and spoonfuls of the cooking liquid.
