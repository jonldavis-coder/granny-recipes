Sides

Skillet Creamed Corn

A short side dish with a spoonable, milky sauce. Fresh or frozen corn both work.

10 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 6

No ratings yet

No favorites yet

Illustration: original artwork for Granny Recipes.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 4 cups corn kernels
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 1 tablespoon chopped chives

Steps

  1. Melt the butter in a wide skillet over medium heat. Add the corn, salt, pepper, and sugar.
  2. Cook, stirring now and then, until the corn looks glossy, about 5 minutes.
  3. Sprinkle the flour over the corn and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in the milk and cream. Simmer gently, stirring often, until the sauce thickens enough to coat a spoon, about 10 minutes.
  5. Taste for salt. Stir in the chives and serve warm.
