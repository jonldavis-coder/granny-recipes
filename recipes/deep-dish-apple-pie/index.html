Deep-Dish Apple Pie

A tall pie with a sturdy crust, meant to be sliced after it has settled so the juices stay in the wedge.

40 min prep · 55 min cook · Serves 8

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon sugar, plus 1 teaspoon for the top
  • 1 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 6 to 8 tablespoons ice water
  • 2 1/2 pounds tart apples, peeled and sliced
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon nutmeg
  • 2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon butter, cut in dots
  • 1 egg, beaten with 1 teaspoon water

Steps

  1. Stir the flour, salt, and 1 tablespoon sugar. Cut in the butter until the mixture looks like coarse crumbs. Sprinkle in ice water a spoon at a time until the dough holds together when pressed.
  2. Divide the dough in half, flatten each half into a disk, wrap, and chill at least 30 minutes.
  3. Heat the oven to 400°F. Toss the apples with brown sugar, lemon juice, cinnamon, nutmeg, and cornstarch.
  4. Roll one dough disk and fit it into a deep 9-inch pie plate. Fill it with the apples and dot with butter.
  5. Roll the second disk, lay it over the fruit, and crimp the edges. Cut four vents in the top. Brush with the egg wash and sprinkle with the remaining sugar.
  6. Bake 20 minutes, then lower the heat to 350°F and bake 30 to 35 minutes more, until the crust is brown and the juices bubble thickly at the vents.
  7. Cool at least 2 hours before slicing so the filling can set.
