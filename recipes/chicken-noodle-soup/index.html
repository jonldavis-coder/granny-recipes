Old-Fashioned Chicken Noodle Soup

A clear, peppery soup for a cold afternoon. The noodles go in at the end so they do not drink up all the broth.

25 min prep · 50 min cook · Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter or oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 3 carrots, sliced
  • 3 celery stalks, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 8 cups chicken broth
  • 1 pound boneless chicken thighs
  • 6 ounces wide egg noodles
  • 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

Steps

  1. Warm the butter in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion, carrots, and celery with a pinch of salt until the onion looks soft, about 8 minutes.
  2. Stir in the garlic and thyme for 30 seconds, then add the bay leaf, broth, chicken, salt, and pepper.
  3. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cook until the chicken is tender, about 25 minutes.
  4. Lift the chicken out, shred it with two forks, and return it to the pot. Discard the bay leaf.
  5. Raise the heat so the soup boils lightly. Stir in the noodles and cook until they are tender, usually 7 to 9 minutes.
  6. Taste for salt and pepper. Turn off the heat, stir in the parsley, and let the pot sit 5 minutes before serving.
