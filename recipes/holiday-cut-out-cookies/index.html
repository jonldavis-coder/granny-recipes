Holiday Cut-Out Sugar Cookies

A sturdy dough for shaped cookies. It chills before rolling so the edges stay sharp in the oven.

25 min prep · 10 min cook · Serves 24

Ingredients

  • 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla
  • 1/2 teaspoon almond extract, optional
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tablespoons milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla, for the icing
  • Sprinkles, if you like

Steps

  1. Whisk the flour and salt. In a large bowl, beat the butter and sugar until creamy. Beat in the egg, vanilla, and almond extract.
  2. Add the flour mixture and mix on low until a soft dough forms. Divide in half, flatten into disks, wrap, and chill at least 1 hour.
  3. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line two sheet pans with parchment.
  4. Roll the dough 1/4 inch thick on a floured counter. Cut shapes and set them 1 inch apart on the pans. Reroll scraps once.
  5. Bake 8 to 10 minutes, until the edges just turn gold. Cool on the pan 5 minutes, then move to a rack.
  6. Stir the powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla into a thick icing. Spread a thin coat on cooled cookies and add sprinkles while the icing is wet.
