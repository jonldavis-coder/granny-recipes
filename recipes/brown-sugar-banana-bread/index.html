Brown Sugar Banana Bread

A one-bowl loaf for bananas that have gone speckled. It stays moist on the second day.

15 min prep · 55 min cook · Serves 10

No favorites yet

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 2/3 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/3 cup plain yogurt or sour cream
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts, optional

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Butter a 9-by-5-inch loaf pan and line the bottom with parchment.
  2. Stir the bananas, melted butter, brown sugar, egg, vanilla, and yogurt until smooth.
  3. Sprinkle the flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon over the bowl. Fold just until no dry streaks remain. Fold in the walnuts if using.
  4. Scrape the batter into the pan and smooth the top.
  5. Bake 50 to 58 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  6. Cool in the pan 15 minutes, then turn out onto a rack. Slice when the loaf is just warm or fully cool.
