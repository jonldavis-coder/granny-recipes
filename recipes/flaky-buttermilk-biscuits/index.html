Flaky Buttermilk Biscuits

A weekend bread for soups and breakfast plates. The dough stays shaggy on purpose so the layers stay tall.

20 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 8

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour, plus a little for the counter
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 6 tablespoons cold unsalted butter, cut in small cubes
  • 3/4 cup cold buttermilk, plus 1 tablespoon for brushing

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Stir the flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and sugar in a wide bowl.
  3. Toss in the butter and pinch it into the flour until the largest bits look like peas.
  4. Pour in the cold buttermilk and stir with a fork until a rough dough forms. Stop while you still see floury patches.
  5. Tip the dough onto a floured counter. Pat it into a 1-inch-thick rectangle, fold it in thirds, and pat it out again. Repeat the fold once more.
  6. Cut straight down into 8 squares. Set them close together on the pan and brush the tops with buttermilk.
  7. Bake 13 to 15 minutes, until the tops are deep gold and the sides look dry. Cool 5 minutes before splitting.
