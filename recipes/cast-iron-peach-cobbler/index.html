Cast-Iron Peach Cobbler

Juicy fruit under a soft biscuit lid. Frozen peaches are fine; thaw them and keep the juice.

20 min prep · 40 min cook · Serves 8

Ingredients

  • 6 cups sliced peaches
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup sugar, for the topping
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 4 tablespoons cold butter, cubed
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Toss the peaches with 1/2 cup sugar, lemon juice, cornstarch, and cinnamon. Spread them in a 10-inch cast-iron skillet or a 2-quart baking dish.
  2. Stir the flour, 1/4 cup sugar, baking powder, and salt. Cut in the butter until the mixture is crumbly.
  3. Stir in the milk and vanilla to make a thick batter. Spoon it in mounds over the peaches, leaving a few gaps for steam.
  4. Bake 35 to 40 minutes, until the topping is golden and the fruit bubbles at the edges.
  5. Cool 20 minutes. Serve warm, on its own or with a spoon of cream.
