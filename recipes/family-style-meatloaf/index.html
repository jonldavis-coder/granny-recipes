Family-Style Meatloaf

A simple loaf with a tangy glaze. A short rest after baking keeps the slices from crumbling.

20 min prep · 1 hr cook · Serves 6

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 small onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 1/2 pounds ground beef
  • 1/2 cup dry breadcrumbs
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon yellow mustard

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a sheet pan with foil and set a rack on it, or use a loaf pan.
  2. Warm the oil in a small skillet and cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds, then cool slightly.
  3. In a large bowl, gently mix the beef, breadcrumbs, milk, egg, Worcestershire sauce, salt, pepper, oregano, and the onion.
  4. Shape the mixture into a loaf about 9 inches long on the rack, or pack it into a loaf pan.
  5. Stir the ketchup, brown sugar, and mustard. Spread half over the loaf.
  6. Bake 45 minutes. Spread the remaining glaze on top and bake 12 to 15 minutes more, until the center reaches 160°F.
  7. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.
